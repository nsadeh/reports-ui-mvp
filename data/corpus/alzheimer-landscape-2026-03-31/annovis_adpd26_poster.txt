ABSTRACT SUBMISSION #3582
A DUAL 6-MONTH & 18-MONTH RANDOMIZED, PLACEBO-CONTROLLED, DOUBLE-BLIND,
CLINICAL TRIAL INVESTIGATING EFFICACY AND SAFETY OF BUNTANETAP IN EARLY ALZHEIMER'S
PARTICIPANTS
POSTER ON BOARD - POSTER ON BOARD SHIFT 01: β-AMYLOID DISEASES / DRUG
DEVELOPMENT, CLINICAL TRIALS / NEW CLINICAL TRIAL DESIGNS; SIMULATION OF PROGRESSDIGITAL TWINS
Cheng Fang, Melissa Gaines, Sarah Mccallum, Maria Maccecchini
Annovis Bio, Malvern, United States of America
Aims: In a Phase 2b dose-ranging study in participants with mild to moderate Alzheimer’s disease
(AD) (NCT05686044), buntanetap (also known as Posiphen) demonstrated a statistically significant
cognitive improvement in plasma pTau217-positive participants with mild AD. These cognitive benefits
were supported by biomarker findings showing that buntanetap reduced neurotoxic aggregation
proteins, decreased inflammation, and improved neuronal function. To validate these effects, a new
pivotal clinical trial in early AD was initiated in April 2025 (NCT06709014).
Methods: A prospective, double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled Phase 3 study is currently
ongoing. A total of 760 participants with early AD will be enrolled across 83 sites in the United States.
This dual- study will include efficacy readout at both 6-month and 18-month, to evaluate buntanetap’s
symptomatic and disease-modifying treatment effects.
Results: We will present updates on site activation status, screening procedures, screen failure rates
and key drivers, enrollment pace, and the projected timeline for database lock.
Conclusions: In summary, the Phase 3 study in early AD is ongoing. We will share our progress to
date, along with key insights and lessons learned throughout the conduct of the trial.
