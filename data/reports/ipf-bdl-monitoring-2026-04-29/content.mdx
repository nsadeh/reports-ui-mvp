This report covers five business development, licensing, and financing events in the Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis (IPF) therapeutic area tracked from March 1 through April 29, 2026. The events involve five sponsors (Avalyn Pharma, Baseimmune, Cumberland Pharmaceuticals Inc., Insilico Medicine, and Rein Therapeutics, Inc.) and span public offerings, a strategic therapeutic expansion, a major branded-portfolio divestiture, and an AI drug discovery company's annual results highlighting its IPF clinical asset. No approved therapies are included in this monitoring period; all covered programs are in preclinical or clinical development.

---

## Executive Summary of Key Events

**Public Offering & Financing**

- **Avalyn Pharma (April 29, 2026):** Avalyn updated its IPO filing to raise approximately $283 million, a 41% increase from its prior filing, allocating $150 million to advance AP01 (inhaled pirfenidone) through Phase 2b and into Phase 3, $90 million for AP02 (inhaled nintedanib) into Phase 3, and $10 million to initiate Phase 1 for AP03. Net proceeds and existing capital are expected to fund operations into 2029.

- **Rein Therapeutics, Inc. (April 28, 2026):** Rein Therapeutics announced a public offering targeting approximately $44.8 million in net proceeds to fund operations through at least April 2027. LTI-03, the company's lead IPF candidate, is currently enrolling patients across approximately 50 sites in the RENEW Phase 2 trial, with initial interim topline data expected in Q4 2026.

**Strategic Shift**

- **Baseimmune (March 12, 2026):** Baseimmune announced a strategic expansion into fibrosis with a preclinical IPF lead program built on computational protein design and multi-pathway immune modulation. No asset name or financial terms were disclosed; key proof-of-concept efficacy readouts are anticipated in 2026 and 2027.

**Licensing & Partnerships**

- **Cumberland Pharmaceuticals Inc. (April 22, 2026):** Cumberland agreed to sell its branded pharmaceutical portfolio to an Apotex affiliate for $100 million, retaining its pipeline including ifetroban, which is in a Phase II program for IPF. The transaction sharpens Cumberland's focus on orphan drug candidates.

**Clinical Development & Deal Value**

- **Insilico Medicine (March 29, 2026):** Insilico Medicine's Phase IIa GENESIS-IPF study of ISM001-055 (Rentosertib), a TNIK inhibitor, demonstrated dose-dependent lung function improvement; results were published in *Nature Medicine*. An inhalable formulation has an IND application submitted, and the company reported US$1.3 billion in new collaborations for 2025.

## Concluding Remarks

This monitoring period covered five business development and financing events across the IPF therapeutic area: Avalyn Pharma's updated IPO filing targeting $283 million to advance its inhaled pirfenidone and nintedanib programmes; Rein Therapeutics' public offering to fund operations through April 2027 while the RENEW Phase 2 trial for LTI-03 continues enrolling; Baseimmune's strategic entry into fibrosis with a preclinical IPF programme built around computational antigen design; Cumberland Pharmaceuticals' $100 million branded-portfolio divestiture to Apotex while retaining its ifetroban IPF pipeline; and Insilico Medicine's 2025 annual results showcasing US$1.3 billion in new collaborations and Phase IIa clinical proof-of-concept for Rentosertib, the first AI-discovered drug to demonstrate dose-dependent lung function improvement in IPF.

This report covers events in the Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis (IPF) therapeutic area tracked from March 1, 2026 through April 29, 2026.
